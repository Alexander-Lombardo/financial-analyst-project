Cash Conversion Cycle Formula

CCC = DSI + DSO - DPO
  • DSI (Days Sales of Inventory): How long inventory sits before being sold
  • DSO (Days Sales Outstanding): How long it takes to collect cash from customers
  • DPO (Days Payables Outstanding): How long before paying suppliers
  • CCC (Cash Conversion Cycle): Total days to convert resources into cash

Lower CCC is better. Negative CCC means collecting cash before paying suppliers (highly efficient).
